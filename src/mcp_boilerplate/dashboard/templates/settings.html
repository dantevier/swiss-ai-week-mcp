Saved to this project's git-ignored .env and never shown again. Leave a field empty to keep its current value.

Free. Crawlora fetches the official Swiss pages for the Refresh buttons. Its free plan gives 2,000 credits a month, no card needed.

  1. Go to crawlora.net and create a free account.
  2. Open your dashboard and copy your API key.
  3. Paste it here and press Save.

Not free, but very cheap. OpenAI has no free API plan; you add a few dollars of prepaid credit. The key powers semantic search (embeddings), which costs a tiny fraction of a cent per query. It is also needed to refresh sources, and benchmark/run_mcp.py uses it to test OpenAI models (--model gpt-5). Without it, search still works with keyword matching.

  1. Sign in at platform.openai.com/api-keys.
  2. Add credit under Billing if your account has none.
  3. Click "Create new secret key", copy it (it is shown only once), paste it here and press Save.

Optional, prepaid. Only used by benchmark/run_mcp.py to test Claude models against the MCP. The benchmark needs this key or the OpenAI key; with both set it uses Claude unless you pass --model.

  1. Sign in at console.anthropic.com/settings/keys.
  2. Add credit under Billing if your account has none.
  3. Click "Create Key", copy it (it is shown only once), paste it here and press Save.
